What this section covers

The hydration section focuses on practical decisions people actually make: how much to drink, when hydration matters more, how workouts change fluid needs, when electrolytes help, and how to build better day-to-day hydration habits.

The goal is not to make hydration look complicated. It is to make it clearer and easier to manage with practical guidance that fits normal life and training.

Who these guides are for

These articles are written for people who want useful hydration guidance without fluff.

  • People trying to stay more consistent with daily water intake.
  • People who train and want to understand hydration before, during, or after workouts.
  • People who want simple explanations around electrolytes, sweat loss, and hydration habits.
  • Users of Water Tracker who want more context behind the habit they are building.

Why hydration content matters here

Hydration is one of the core parts of the Stellar Lift ecosystem, not just a random blog topic.

Water Tracker exists to make hydration logging and reminders easier, and these articles support that by giving readers practical context. That makes this section part of the actual value of the site, not filler around an app page.
