Water Tracker — Drink Reminder: simple hydration tracking for daily consistency

Water Tracker — Drink Reminder is a hydration app designed for people who want quick logging, practical reminders, visible daily goals, and a cleaner way to stay consistent with water intake.

Many hydration apps either feel too basic to be useful or too cluttered to use comfortably every day. Water Tracker is built around a simpler middle ground: make logging fast, keep reminders practical, and make the daily routine easier to repeat.

The point is not to turn hydration into a complicated task. The point is to make it visible, easy, and consistent enough that users can actually stick with it over time.
