Water Tracker — Drink Reminder

A simple hydration tracking app built for reminders, quick logging, and daily consistency.

Water Tracker — Drink Reminder is designed to make hydration tracking fast and easy. Instead of adding friction, the app focuses on simple reminders, quick water logging, and a cleaner daily routine that is easier to maintain over time.

What it is for

Built for people who want a cleaner way to stay on top of hydration.

  • Logging water intake quickly during the day.
  • Using reminders to stay more consistent with hydration.
  • Keeping daily water goals visible and easy to follow.
  • Making hydration tracking simple enough to stick with.

Why it is different

The app focuses on usability instead of overcomplication.

Quick logging

The app is designed to keep water tracking fast so it feels easy to use throughout the day.

Reminder-based consistency

Reminders are there to support the habit, not overwhelm the user with unnecessary complexity.

Cleaner daily routine

The app keeps hydration visible without turning it into a complicated process.

Part of the Stellar Lift ecosystem

It fits into a wider set of focused tools designed around simple health and fitness tracking.

Who it is for

A practical option for people who want hydration tracking without clutter.

  • People trying to improve daily water intake.
  • Users who benefit from simple reminders.
  • Anyone who wants a lightweight hydration app.
  • People who prefer focused tools over bloated all-in-one apps.
