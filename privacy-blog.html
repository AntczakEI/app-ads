Who we are

Stellar Lift Blog (“we”, “us”, “our”) is available as part of Stellar Lift and is operated by Antczak Enterprise International (Stellar Lift).

What we collect

  • Basic usage information, such as pages viewed, approximate device/browser information, general location data, and similar analytics signals that help us understand how the site is used.
  • Information you send us if you contact us, such as your email address and message.
  • Advertising and consent-related information, where applicable, to support ad delivery, measure ad performance, and store your consent choices.

How we use information

We use information to operate the website, understand which content is useful, improve performance and user experience, respond to messages, maintain site security, and support advertising where enabled.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site functioning, measure performance, understand traffic, improve content, store consent choices, and support advertising.

Depending on your location, cookies and similar technologies used on this site may include:

  • Essential cookies needed for basic site operation and consent management.
  • Analytics cookies used to understand traffic and improve the website.
  • Advertising cookies used by advertising services such as Google AdSense, subject to applicable consent requirements.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or another region where consent is required, we display a consent message and store your choices. You can also control cookies through your browser settings.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed, how visitors move through the website, and which content performs best. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to collect usage information.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We use Google AdSense to display ads on this site.

  • Google AdSense may use cookies or device identifiers to show ads, measure ad performance, help prevent fraud or abuse, and reduce repeated ads.
  • Depending on your region and your consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.
  • If consent is required in your region, Google AdSense will respect the selection you make in the consent message shown on the site.

Third-party services

Some services used on this website are provided by third parties, and those providers may process data under their own privacy policies.

  • Google Analytics – website analytics and traffic reporting.
  • Google AdSense – advertising, reporting, and fraud prevention.
  • GitHub Pages – hosting and delivery of site content.

Your choices

  • You can manage cookie and advertising preferences through the consent message where available.
  • You can also control or block cookies through your browser settings.
  • If applicable, you can review and adjust Google advertising preferences in your Google account settings.

Data retention

We keep data only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the website, improve the service, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Children

This site is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us via the Contact page or by email: office@antczak.co.uk.

Updates to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the site evolves, services change, or legal requirements are updated. The latest version will always be published on this page.
