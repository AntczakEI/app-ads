Cookies & Ads

What may be used on this site, why it may be used, and how you can control it.

Last updated: 5 April 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files or similar browser-based technologies used to support website functionality, remember choices, understand usage, and help with advertising and measurement.

How this site may use cookies and similar technologies

  • Essential use – to support basic site functions and remember privacy choices.
  • Analytics use – to understand site traffic and improve content.
  • Advertising use – to support ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related ad functions.

Consent and regional rules

If you visit from a region where consent is required, such as the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, the site may display a consent message through a certified consent management platform.

That message may let you choose between:

  • Consent
  • Do not consent
  • Manage options

Your choices affect how advertising and analytics technologies are used on the site where applicable.

Privacy and cookie settings

Advertising and Google AdSense

This site uses Google AdSense.

  • Personalized ads may use cookies or identifiers to make ads more relevant, depending on region and consent.
  • Non-personalized ads do not rely on ad personalization, but may still use limited data for functions such as fraud prevention, frequency capping, and basic reporting.
  • Advertising behavior may differ depending on the privacy choices you make in the consent interface.

Analytics and Google Analytics

This site also uses Google Analytics to understand traffic and improve the website. Analytics use may depend on your privacy choices where required.

How to control cookies

  • Use the consent interface shown on the site, where available.
  • Reopen your choices through the Privacy and cookie settings link when available.
  • Use your browser settings to block, limit, or delete cookies.
  • You may also manage certain Google advertising preferences through your Google account, where available.
