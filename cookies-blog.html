Cookies & Ads

What we use, why we use it, and how you control it.

Last updated: 1 March 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device. They help websites work correctly, improve user experience, and help measure what content performs best.

What we use cookies for

  • Site functionality – basic features, stability, and general site operation.
  • Measurement – understanding what pages people visit and what content to improve.
  • Advertising – showing ads and measuring ad performance (including Google AdSense).

Consent (EEA/UK)

If you are in the EEA/UK (or another region where consent is required), we display a consent message. Your selection helps determine whether ads can be personalized and what measurement is allowed.

Personalized vs non-personalized ads

  • Personalized ads may use cookies or identifiers to show more relevant ads based on your activity or interests.
  • Non-personalized ads do not use ad personalization, but may still use limited signals for fraud prevention, frequency capping, and basic ad reporting.

How to control cookies

  • Use the consent message (where shown) to manage your choices.
  • Use your browser settings to block or delete cookies.
  • If you use Google services, you can manage ad personalization in your Google account settings (where available).
