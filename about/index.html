About Stellar Lift

Simple, focused tools designed to make tracking easier, faster, and more consistent.

Stellar Lift is a growing ecosystem of lightweight health and fitness trackers built around one core idea: tracking should help people stay consistent, not overwhelm them. Instead of forcing everything into one bloated app, Stellar Lift keeps each tool focused on a single job.

The philosophy

Why Stellar Lift is built as separate apps instead of one all-in-one platform.

Single-purpose by design

Each app is meant to do one thing well. That keeps the experience faster, cleaner, and easier to use every day.

Consistency over complexity

The goal is not endless setup or advanced configuration. The goal is helping people build habits they can actually maintain.

Less friction

Open the app, log the action, move on. Fewer taps and less clutter make regular tracking more realistic.

Long-term ecosystem

The wider Stellar Lift roadmap is designed to grow over time, while keeping each individual app focused and lightweight.

What is live now

The current live products in the Stellar Lift ecosystem.

  • Training Tracker — a simple workout logging app focused on speed, consistency, and clear progress.
  • Water Tracker — Drink Reminder — a hydration app built for quick logging, reminders, and daily goal tracking.

How the blog fits in

The blog supports the ecosystem with practical content, not filler.

The Stellar Lift blog expands the ecosystem with practical articles on hydration, training, recovery, and consistency. It is there to support the apps with useful content, simple guidance, and topic-specific reading for people who want more than a download page.
